# Start here

Press play at the bottom, and this page is read to you. The word being spoken lights up, so your eyes and your ears stay together.

## The bar under every word

Click any word and a small bar appears on it.

- Play or pause from that word.
- Step one word back or forward.
- Report a word that sounds wrong, or a word that is missing.

## The bar at the bottom

- Play, pause and stop.
- Skip a word or a whole paragraph in either direction.
- Speed, from three quarters to double, or the exact words per minute.
- Voices: your browser's own for free, better ones you download, or your own key for a provider's voices.
- Sound and Tune: volume, the pauses at lines and paragraphs, and whether a second language on the page gets its own voice.
- Text, Flow and Page: the plain text, one line at a time, or the real page when there is one.

## Keys

- Space: play or pause.
- Left and right arrows: one word. Hold Ctrl for a paragraph.
- Up and down arrows: faster or slower. Hold Ctrl for volume.
- Ctrl and Space: continue reading from the lit word.
- Ctrl and Home: from the beginning.
- M: mute. R: report the lit word.

## Finding your way in a longer file

- The page box at the top jumps anywhere.
- The list icon opens page thumbnails, the table of contents, and your bookmarks.
- Scroll freely; Continue reading brings you back to the lit word.
- Every file opens where you left off.

## Bringing your own

- Open, on the left, takes a PDF, EPUB, Word, Markdown or plain text file. Scanned pages show first, and the words follow.
- Write, on the left, is for anything you paste or type.
- What you open stays on this device, in your Shelf. It is not sent anywhere unless you choose a provider's voice or report a word.

That is all. Open something you have been meaning to read, and listen.
